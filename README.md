## About

Agisoft Metashape is professional photogrammetry software for Windows, macOS, and Linux. Process thousands of photographs into detailed 3D models, orthomosaics, and point clouds for architecture, cultural heritage documentation, forestry, and geospatial applications. Used by researchers, surveyors, and VFX professionals, Metashape produces industry-grade 3D reconstruction results from drone and ground-based photography.

## Common Problems & Limitations

- **Commercial License** — Professional pricing for photogrammetry software
- **Processing Resources** — GPU and RAM intensive; full-resolution processing requires powerful hardware
- **Learning Curve** — Photogrammetry workflow requires understanding of optimal capture conditions
- **Processing Time** — Large datasets with hundreds of photos can take hours to process
